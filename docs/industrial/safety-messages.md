# Safety Messages

## 1. DANGER

**Hazard:** Electric shock hazard.

**Consequence:** Contact with live electrical parts can result in serious injury or death.

**Avoidance:** Disconnect and isolate the power supply before working on the equipment.

## 2. WARNING

**Hazard:** Unexpected equipment operation.

**Consequence:** Unexpected operation may cause injury.

**Avoidance:** Verify that the equipment is in a safe state before performing maintenance.

## 3. NOTICE

**Situation:** Risk of equipment damage.

**Consequence:** Incorrect wiring may damage the gateway.

**Avoidance:** Verify the wiring connections before energizing the equipment.
